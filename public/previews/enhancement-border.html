Lv. 3 · Brighter Aura

Base frames keep their rarity colour and rounded style, with the same thickness and glow at every rarity. Enhancement increases the frame thickness and soft aura: Lv. 1 none, Lv. 2 orange (S), Lv. 3 silver (SS), Lv. 4 gold (SSS), Lv. 5 purple (Ubers), MAX rainbow (AG). Level badges follow D → S → SS → SSS → Ubers → AG. The aura fully surrounds the card and fades gently outwards.

This style is applied to cards and profile pictures throughout Gatcha. Motion inspiration: nazar-gavrylyk on Uiverse.
